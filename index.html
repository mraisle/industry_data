Known limitations. The ~110 Mt "all others" producer figure and the hematite/concentrate volume split are derived from WATC totals, not published breakdowns. The ~7 Mt domestic ore consumption applies a 2011–12 BITRE ore-to-steel ratio to current output, so it is an inference. WATC's per-producer product splits do not reconcile with company-reported shipments; the 4.7% concentrate aggregate is the reliable figure.

On emissions. Route intensities are gate-to-gate per tonne of crude steel and exclude mining, shipping and rolling, so they are not directly comparable with a CBAM embedded-emissions calculation. No operating emissions benchmark exists for the DRI-ESF route; the 0.40 t CO₂-e near-zero threshold is a definition, not a measurement.
